## LinkedIn post

Before reposting a photo or video, what can you learn about its source?

ProvenanceX gives media a public media record on Monad. Creators register a file and sign a captured, synthetic, edited, or unknown source declaration. Anyone can compare a downloaded file with the registered original, inspect its history, and follow linked derivatives. Declared AI/synthetic media is clearly labeled.

Exact hashes check file identity. Image similarity only suggests related files. Video checks use exact hashing, and source declarations remain signer claims; the app does not automatically detect deepfakes or establish the truth of a post.
